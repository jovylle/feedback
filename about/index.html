About this feedback page

This is a small static feedback portal for ideas, bugs, and requests. In your case it can be framed as the feedback inbox for your Sunflower Land digging assistant tool.

  • Quick feedback form with no account required
  • Supports query params like ?context=sunflower-land-digging-assistant
  • Safety-first: stay on the page during submission

If you load /?context=..., that slug is attached to the submission and shown as page context.

Recommended link for your tool: https://feedback.uft1.com/?context=sunflower-land-digging-assistant

The label is derived from the slug automatically, so ?context=chat-widget becomes Chat widget and ?context=portfolio becomes Portfolio.

Backward compatible: the old ?for=... link still works.
